# Arenas and effects
Three generated arenas share a modular tile/brazier kit, with columns/clouds, terraces/crowd, or basalt/lava. MatchConfig chooses arena and hazards. Safe zone is -2200..2200 mm; edge hazards are outside it. Seeded scheduling starts in the future, giving 90 complete warning ticks. Lightning deals 25 main/18 limb damage and a 300-tick char marker; lava deals 12/5. Athens has no hazard. One hit per fighter per eruption; hazard damage does not increment combo chains.

Cosmetic RigidBody debris uses collision layer 2 and never feeds simulation. Fifteen original chunk meshes each use 20 triangles. Hard limits: 24 debris bodies, 16 particle/ring effects, 64 floor and 32 wall splats. Oldest entries are recycled; splats fade, faster on sand. Off uses dust/sparks, Low/Normal/Max scale counts; red/green/golden blood is supported. FightCamera frames the two bodies and clamps its center, with capped optional shake and sever zoom. Compatibility renderer uses flat splats, not decals.

Debug: scenes/debug/arena_viewer.tscn. Headless determinism: tools/sim/hazard_replay.gd. Visuals remain original procedural stylized assets, not commercial-quality scanned characters. Gameplay movement curve deliberately changed to [0,12,22,30,38,46] mm/tick at owner request; replay baselines were regenerated after exact movement tests and same-input determinism passed.

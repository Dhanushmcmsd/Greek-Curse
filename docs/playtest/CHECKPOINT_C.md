# Checkpoint C — owner playtest

Run `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\tools\playtest_checkpoint_c.ps1` from the project folder, or open project.godot in the pinned Godot 4.7.2 and press F6 on scenes/menus/main.tscn. The launcher uses a separate persistent playtest profile. Estimated review: 45–60 minutes. Return PASS or specific bugs/balance notes; screenshots and the selected fighter/mode/limb help reproduce them.

1. Accept the courtesy age gate/content warning. Navigate main menu, setup, settings, loadout, back and results using keyboard; repeat with a gamepad if available. Pages should replace one another without the old result text showing through. Try 200% text and scroll to every action.
2. Play each of Theron, Atalanta, Pyrrhus and Sibyl across Olympus, Athens and Tartarus. Check facing after crossing, quicker movement, distinct walking/guard/attack/recoil poses, visible blocked attacks and effects. Are strikes readable and comfortable? Do attacks visually connect? Current art is original stylized procedural 3D, not photorealistic or motion-captured Tekken/Mortal Kombat quality.
3. Open Replacement limbs during a Fresh Body/Practice fight. Check the actual-model cards and full descriptions. Test chicken light reach/heavy failure, giant-hand damage/large target, claw grab through block/miss pinch, marble toughness/slower movement and kick, Hermes speed/dash/random zoom. Replacements start a new fight here. Repeated limb hits should visibly bruise/crack/sever; check detached limb collision, stump cap, blood effect and all-limbs-missing movement.
4. Test block impact, combos, camera shake, ultimate startup and both ultimates through Loadout. U is superpower and I ultimate on keyboard P1. The Controls page lists/remaps all bindings. Try missing arms/legs with supers and body attacks.
5. Create Career; win and select an offer; lose a limb; trade an earned non-starter ability with Obol. Confirm the removed ability and new limb. Quit/relaunch/Continue and verify the same body, record and loadout. Optional retirement is permanent and explicitly confirmed. Do not retire a fighter you want to keep.
6. Try Fresh Body, keyboard or two-controller Local Versus, and Practice. Try Rematch and New match. With hazards enabled, edge warnings should precede strikes and the central area should be safe. Athens has no damaging hazard.
7. Change gore Off/Low/Normal/Max, red/green/golden blood, shake, hit-stop, flash reduction, captions, contrast, text scale and bindings. Restart to check persistence. Test display Keep and automatic ten-second Revert. Gore Off should hide blood/bone presentation while preserving gameplay.
8. Listen for around ten minutes. Report overly loud, repetitive or unpleasant generated placeholder audio. Report strongest/weakest fighter and abilities and whether several matches remain fun. Balance is provisional; see docs/balance/report_2026-10-24.md.

Automation already checks gameplay invariants, replays, body/limb combinations, generated asset budgets/reproducibility, saves/recovery and menu graph/rendering. It cannot decide whether the animation, controls, audio and fighting feel meet your expectations. This is why work pauses here before stage 21. No packaged release or owner PASS is claimed.

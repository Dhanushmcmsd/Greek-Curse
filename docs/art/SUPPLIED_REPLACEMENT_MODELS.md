## 2. Model Structure, Hierarchy & Format

Each character directory in `d:\Elemental_Limbs_10_Characters\Elemental_Limbs` contains:
- `*_arm_R.fbx` & `*_leg_R.fbx`: Independent limb exports with embedded textures and ancestral Mixamo deform skeletons.
- `*_arm_R.glb` & `*_leg_R.glb`: Binary glTF format with embedded PBR textures (`BaseColor`, `Emission`, `Roughness`).
- `*_limbs.blend`: Authoring scene containing both separate limb meshes, full Mixamo armature, and packed image textures.
- `arm_textures/` & `leg_textures/`: 1024×1024 BaseColor, Emission, and Roughness PNGs.
- `asset_manifest.json`: Rig bindings, bind matrices, vertex counts, and geometry checksums.

### Anatomical & Connection Framing:
- **Arm Replacements (`*_arm_R`):** Complete right arm branch (shoulder cut at `mixamorig:RightArm`, including upper arm, forearm, hand, and all 5 individual fingers).
- **Leg Replacements (`*_leg_R`):** Complete right leg branch (hip cut at `mixamorig:RightUpLeg`, including thigh, shin, foot, and toes).
- **Side:** Native exports are **Right side only (`_R`)**. Mirrored left-side equivalents (`_L`) are generated by flipping across the sagittal plane (X-axis) and binding to the character's left-side joint chains (`mixamorig:LeftArm` / `mixamorig:LeftUpLeg`).
- **Units & Coordinate System:** Mixamo ancestral FBX centimeter scale (armature world matrix scale 0.01 with 90° X-rotation). In Godot and Blender fighter space, converted to 1 unit = 1 meter matching the character deform skeleton.
